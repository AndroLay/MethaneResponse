## Camera and attention rules

- Establish the workspace, zoom into a meaningful target, hold through its result, then restore context.
- Use a fractional affine transform and quintic easing; avoid integer crop stepping, decorative drift, spring overshoot, and moving the camera while the page scrolls.
- Focus and highlight positions use real DOM bounds. The pointer uses actual timestamped browser events, minimum-jerk travel, dwell before clicking, a white arrow, and a restrained teal click ripple.
- A shared 16:9 application window connects explanation and demo. Its first/last recorded frames are retained across the transition. It expands into the demo and contracts into the next explanation; no unrelated directional wipe is used.
- Typography and explanatory diagrams are rendered every frame. They are not three pre-rendered slide states dissolved together.
