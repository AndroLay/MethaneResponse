## Story spine

1. **Why:** methane is a major climate driver; a response statistic does not establish repair or support for a specific mitigation claim.
2. **What:** MethaneResponse adds a traceable, claim-scoped review workflow around historical MARS information.
3. **How:** select KAZ_S_159, inspect the official UNEP catalogue metadata and discrete EnMAP observations, apply the July 11 event scope, run the deterministic rules baseline, record a local evidence request, and verify an exported report.
4. **Proof:** the app reproduces a bundle-bounded result and detects a deliberately changed report copy. These checks do not prove physical mitigation, document authenticity, or a trusted issuer.

## Current limits

- MARS data are historical, not a live feed.
- The UNEP KAZ item is represented by its attributed title, abstract metadata, and link. The linked report body is not included or independently reassessed here.
- The rules baseline returns insufficient evidence for the submitted, date-bounded bundle; it does not contradict UNEP or establish that mitigation failed.
- Voice is generated locally with Kokoro and is synthetic, not a human recording. Subjective naturalness still needs human listening.
- There is no live AI provider or public-chain receipt in the captured preview. No emissions avoided or reviewer time saved has been measured.
- A matching local hash proves consistency with the hash carried in the file; it does not authenticate an issuer or establish physical truth.
