## Production guidance

- [Product narrative](2026-10-21-current-product-narrative.md): historical 4:50 treatment connecting capacity, document assessment, reviewer decisions, snapshot changes and contract verification.
- [Production stack](2026-10-21-video-production-stack.md): roles, tools, source continuity, sound, capture and export requirements.
- [UI review](2026-10-21-ui-before-video-review.md): initial findings, subsequent fixes and verification boundaries.
- [Archived Notion materials](notion/README.md): local presentation references. Importing or publishing a Notion page is not required.

Use actual app footage and verifiable source identifiers. Label historical data,
DEMO claims/reviewers, recorded model examples and Anvil local chain explicitly.
Do not turn missing evidence into a claim of failed repair, zero emissions,
measured productivity or proven climate benefit.
