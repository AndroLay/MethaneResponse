## 2. Final concept

**One explainable investigation plan. One actionable evidence request.
One verifiable decision.**

A team has thousands of records and limited capacity. MethaneResponse explains
what to investigate, turns evidence gaps into precise requests and preserves a
reviewable decision. UNEP MARS already detects, alerts and supports response.
This prototype adds investigation planning and evidence workflows over historical
records; it is not replacement detection, live surveillance or repair certification.

Answer: What do we investigate? What evidence do we need? Can another reviewer check
our decision? Show IDs, snapshot, UTC scope, uncertainty, exact quote and trust anchor
when relevant. Do not begin with technology lists or test totals.

## 9. Subtitles, audio, motion and transitions

Professional documentary style, navy/cyan, one message per composition. Cursor 24–30 px
at 1080p, moves smoothly to one target, pauses 150–250 ms before actual click. Click rings
follow real action timestamps. Keep scroll stable while typing; do not chase the
cursor every frame. Focus zoom 1.15–1.35×,450–650 ms easing, 2–4 s reading hold, then
restore context before navigation. Preserve source/environment badges.

results are ready. TKM↔KAZ needs a clearly visible label/card for at least 2 s; never
hide a case change with a seamless transition. Relevant labels:

`TKM EXPORT · BEFORE ANCHOR`, `SAME EXPORT · ISOLATED BACKEND REPLAY`.
Use readable 24–28 px labels, not microscopic disclaimers.

Audition an adult male English TTS voice with calm, conversational delivery. Synthetic
speech is not a real narrator; do not imitate a named person. Available `edge-tts`
is a starting point, not verified naturalness. Target 125–145 wpm, 0.2–0.5 s phrase
pauses and reading space. Rewrite overrunning cues without deleting limits; avoid
aggressive time stretching. Duck original instrumental beneath speech. Initial mix
target−18 LUFS±1.5, true peak≤−2 dBTP, stereo 48 kHz. English captions: two lines max,
approximately 42 characters/line, high-contrast dark panel; align to final audio, not draft
slots. Preserve IDs, numbers and negation. Do not cover quotes/source labels.
