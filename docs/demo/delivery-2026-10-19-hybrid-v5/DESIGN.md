# Visual identity — hybrid presentation v5

## Purpose

Make a climate-data judge understand, in one short film, what UNEP MARS does, what MethaneResponse adds, and what the displayed KAZ_S_159 review can and cannot establish. The film alternates authored explanation with actual application footage. The graphics clarify evidence; they never stand in for evidence.

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** 1920 × 1080, 16:9, with 96 px outer safe margins.
- **Core palette:** ink `#071723`, deep teal `#0B2935`, pale paper `#F5F8F7`, white `#FFFFFF`, brand teal `#55D8C5`, evidence amber `#F1B955`, caution coral `#E88376`, muted blue-gray `#A8BBC1`.
- **Meaning:** teal marks workflow and source navigation; amber highlights a selected observation or its uncertainty; coral marks a failed integrity check. Color never encodes an unreported methane concentration.
- **Type:** Inter for titles and body; DejaVu Sans Mono for IDs, UTC dates, units, and digests.
- **Source line:** fixed, legible attribution at the bottom of explainer scenes. Keep it separate from captions.

## Motion language

- Chapters progress through **Context → MARS → Case → Review → Integrity → Limits**.
- Use a moving coordinate/connector motif to carry attention from the map into the case and from the evidence bundle into the report.
- Build diagrams in reading order. Reveal observation points and uncertainty ranges one at a time; do not connect sparse observations with a line.
- Give each scene one focal action and a quiet reading hold. Use scale changes and match cuts only when the same record, date, or report is retained across the cut.
- Actual application footage remains legible and unaltered. Overlays may identify an action or limitation but must not cover its result.

## Do not use

- Generated methane plumes, invented satellite imagery, fake company statements, or invented operator actions.
- A continuous flux trend between three discrete observations.
- “Live” labels, a public-chain receipt, or an AI-provider result that is not in the captured preview.
- Animation that implies the July 11 observation happened after the mitigation action; the catalog gives only the month.
- Visuals, logos, music, or footage copied from the four reference videos.
