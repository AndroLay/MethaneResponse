## Motion language

- Slide-to-application transitions use short dissolves; no directional wipe is used.
- Focus moves use eased zoom, a readable hold, and a pull-back. The camera rests between close-ups and does not move concurrently with page scrolling.
- Authored slides keep a locked camera; diagram, annotation, and chart elements provide the movement.
- The output cadence matches the recorded app capture at 25 fps, avoiding a 25-to-30 fps conversion.
- Every zoom is composed from the recorded 1920×1080 application capture. The local interaction clip is captured from the browser at 1920×1080; its cursor marker is an overlay driven by actual Playwright pointer events. No separate crop is presented as new scientific evidence.
