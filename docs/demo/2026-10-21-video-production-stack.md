## Sequential workflow

1. **Lock story/claims.** Bind every outcome statement to a source, status
   (historical/DEMO/observed/proposed) and shot. Distinguish potential from measured
   climate benefit; explain urgency without a long atmospheric-science lecture.
2. **Preflight continuity.** Check selected source, snapshot, claim, period and authority
   across assessment→decision. Prior model proof uses KAZ_S_159; never disguise it
   as TKM_S_021. Prefer labeled existing proof over unnecessary paid calls. A newly
   authorized model run needs legitimate DEMO excerpts and an explicit usage limit.
   Local chain 31337 requires no public funds. Check document/font/basemap/music
   rights, disk space and quota.
3. **Capture actual actions.** Use disposable state and real target elements. Record
   source IDs, environment, action/shot clocks, exports and trusted digest. Keep
   credentials outside frame; do not fake unavailable interactions.
4. **Motion study.** Test 20–35 seconds of the densest section before full production.
   Cursor leads the action; camera focuses the actual target, holds reading context
   and returns before navigation. Avoid cursor-follow jitter.
5. **Narration/sound.** Audition male voice, measure each cue, revise text rather than
   stretching speech; retain scientific limits. Align word timing, captions and music
   ducking to final audio.
6. **Assembly/master.** Use approved footage and diagrams; presentation↔demo transitions
   must preserve context. Keep all transitions inside shot duration. Maintain a
   source/rights ledger and final media hashes.
7. **Verification.** Inspect actual rendered frames, metadata, decode, audio loudness,
   subtitle sync, privacy, case continuity and readable quotes/results. Full normal-speed
   playback/listening is required to evaluate pronunciation and pacing. Software
   checks alone do not establish artistic quality.
8. **Delivery/publication.** Keep local MP4, GitHub source, public deployment, video upload
   and Devpost submission separate. External publication needs applicable approval;
   verify public links anonymously afterward.

## Visual/audio standards

Navy/cyan documentary style; one idea per screen. Original diagrams explain processes,
not fabricated observations. Cursor 24–30 px at 1080p, 150–250 ms pre-click pause;
450–650 ms eased zoom, 2–4 second reading hold, stable scroll. Keep source/environment
labels visible. Case changes need explicit labels, not deceptive seamless editing.
English subtitles: two lines, approximately 42 characters/line, high contrast, do not
cover evidence. Target 125–145 spoken words/minute with 0.2–0.5 second phrase pauses.
Initial mix−18 LUFS±1.5, true peak≤−2 dBTP, 48 kHz stereo; measure the actual master.

No generated satellite/plume evidence, fake operator response, borrowed receipt,
false live-feed label or claim of measured productivity/repair/emissions reduction.
