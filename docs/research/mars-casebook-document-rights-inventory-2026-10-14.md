### License interpretation and runner safeguards

The [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits sharing/adaptation subject to attribution, a license link, change notices, and no implied endorsement; it also cautions that a license may not grant every permission needed for a particular use. A visible CC BY 4.0 statement is therefore a promising rights basis for the article text, but it does not itself verify provider retention/training settings, third-party figures, privacy, or scientific validity. Candidate records above remain unapproved until a reviewer records the exact requested scopes.

The benchmark runner now fails closed unless each submitted document has a reviewer/date, stable source URL, named license or private permission reference, and explicit approvals for excerpt processing, provider processing, private evaluation storage, quote storage, and metrics publication. It hashes this rights review into each private prediction; the evaluator requires the same digest on the adjudicated gold row. This is tamper-evident metadata, not proof of reviewer identity, legal permission, or the accuracy of a license interpretation. Public quotation publication remains a separate consent decision.
