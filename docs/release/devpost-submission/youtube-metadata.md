## Description

```text
How should an investigation team decide which methane sources to examine—and what evidence is needed before accepting a claim of repair?

MethaneResponse helps environmental analysts, researchers, regulators, and independent auditors turn historical satellite observations into a clear investigation plan, a specific evidence request, and a decision that others can examine.

Developed for the IEEE ClimateChain Global Hackathon, the prototype builds on historical UNEP MARS records. It brings source observations, investigation priorities, document assessment, and review history into one workflow: Plan → Investigate → Verify.

This demonstration shows how a team compares investigation capacity, examines a source, identifies missing evidence, records follow-up requirements, and checks a saved decision against a cryptographic commitment.

AI assists with document assessment and checkable quotations; a reviewer remains responsible for the decision. The blockchain demonstration uses Anvil, a local Ethereum development chain, to detect changes to a report. It verifies integrity, not physical repair.

The main investigation uses historical Turkmenistan data. A separate Kazakhstan example shows recorded DeepSeek assistance on a project-authored demonstration excerpt. Reviewer actions and follow-up responses are labeled DEMO. No emissions reduction or improvement in human productivity has been measured.
```
